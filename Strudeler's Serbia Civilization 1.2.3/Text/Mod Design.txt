CIVILIZATION 1: Serbia (Empire of Serbia/Serbian Empire)

LEADER 1: Stefan Uros IV Dusan the Mighty Nemanjic

CIVILIZATION 1 TRAIT: Sclaveni
-Miltary Units gain +4 Combat Strength during a Dark Age.
-Religious Units gain +1 Movement during a Dark Age.
-Settlers gain +1 Movement during a Golden Age.
-Settling Cities during a Golden Age grants +2 Population.

LEADER 1 TRAIT: Dusan's Code
-Defeating an enemy Unit grants +1 Era Score.
-Conquered Cities do not lose Population.
-Conquered Cities gain +7 Loyalty.

LEADER 1 AGENDA: Emperor of Serbs and Greeks
-Likes civilizations that are in a Golden Age.
-Dislikes civilizations that are in a Dark Age.

CIVILIZATION 1 UNIT: Gusar
-Replaces the Courser.
-Gains bonuses that would effect a Naval Ranged class Unit.
- 1 Range.
-Can melee attack when on land.
-Pillaging grants an additional 50 Gold.
-Can fight while at sea.
-Can Coastal Raid when on coast tiles.

CIVILIZATION 1 IMPROVEMENT: Autocephalous Church
- +1 Housing.
- +2 Loyalty.
- +1 Faith.
-Gain standard adjacency based on what Specialty Districts are adjacent, this becomes major adjacency with the Nationalism Civic.
-Religious Units heal +20 Health when fortified on the tile.
-A City can only have 1 Autcephalous Church and cannot swap its tile.
